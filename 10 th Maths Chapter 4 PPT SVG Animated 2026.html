Ex 4.1 - Q 9 Two vertical poles of heights $6$ m and $3$ m are erected above a horizontal ground $AC$. Find the value of $y$.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 4.1 Question 9 Crossed Wires / Poles Theorem
Must-Use Formula By similar triangles $\triangle PAC \sim \triangle QBC$ and $\triangle RCA \sim \triangle QBA$: $\dfrac{1}{y}$ $= \dfrac{1}{h_1} + \dfrac{1}{h_2}$
Step 1
Use Similar Triangles $\triangle PAC \sim \triangle QBC$:
Since $PA \perp AC$ and $QB \perp AC$, $\triangle PAC \sim \triangle QBC$ $\implies \dfrac{QB}{PA} = \dfrac{BC}{AC}$ $\implies \dfrac{y}{6} = \dfrac{BC}{AC}$ $\cdots (1)$.
Step 2
Use Similar Triangles $\triangle RCA \sim \triangle QBA$:
Since $RC \perp AC$ and $QB \perp AC$, $\triangle RCA \sim \triangle QBA$ $\implies \dfrac{QB}{RC} = \dfrac{AB}{AC}$ $\implies \dfrac{y}{3} = \dfrac{AB}{AC}$ $\cdots (2)$.
Step 3
Add Equations $(1)$ and $(2)$:
$\dfrac{y}{6} + \dfrac{y}{3}$ $= \dfrac{BC + AB}{AC}$ $= \dfrac{AC}{AC}$ $= 1$ $\implies \dfrac{y + 2y}{6} = 1$ $\implies \dfrac{3y}{6} = 1$ $\implies y = 2$ m.
Answer: $y = 2$ m.
Two Vertical Poles ($6$ m & $3$ m): Intersection Height $y = 2$ m
