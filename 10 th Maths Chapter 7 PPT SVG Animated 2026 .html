⭐ MUST-USE MENSURATION FORMULA CHEAT SHEET (CHAPTER 7 — CSA, TSA & VOLUME) Use $\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}$ unless specified
1. Right Circular Cylinder & Hollow Cylinder Solid: $\text{CSA} = 2\pi rh$,   $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(h+r)$,   $\text{Vol} = \pi r^2 h$
Hollow: $\text{CSA} = 2\pi(R+r)h$,   $\text{TSA} = 2\pi(R+r)(R-r+h)$,   $\text{Vol} = \pi(R^2-r^2)h$
2. Right Circular Cone Slant Height: $l = \sqrt{r^2+h^2}$,   $r = \sqrt{l^2-h^2}$,   $h = \sqrt{l^2-r^2}$
Areas & Vol: $\text{CSA} = \pi rl$,   $\text{TSA} = \pi r(l+r)$,   $\text{Volume} = \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$
3. Sphere & Hemisphere (Solid & Hollow) Sphere: $\text{SA} = 4\pi r^2$,   $\text{Vol} = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$,   Hollow Sphere Vol: $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(R^3-r^3)$
Hemisphere: $\text{CSA} = 2\pi r^2$, $\text{TSA} = 3\pi r^2$, $\text{Vol} = \dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3$, Hollow TSA: $\pi(3R^2+r^2)$
4. Frustum of a Right Circular Cone Slant Height: $l = \sqrt{h^2+(R-r)^2}$  |  CSA: $\pi(R+r)l$
TSA: $\pi(R+r)l + \pi R^2 + \pi r^2$  |  Volume: $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi h(R^2+Rr+r^2)$

Ex 7.1 - Q 1 The radius and height of a cylinder are in the ratio $5:7$ and its curved surface area is $5500\text{ sq.cm}$. Find its radius and height.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 1 Right Circular Cylinder
Must-Use Formula CSA of Right Circular Cylinder: $\text{CSA} = 2\pi rh\text{ sq. units}$
Step 1
Given Ratio: Radius $:$ Height $= r : h = 5 : 7$.
Let the radius $r = 5x\text{ cm}$ and height $h = 7x\text{ cm}$.
Step 2
Apply CSA Formula ($2\pi rh = 5500$):
$2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 5x \times 7x$ $= 5500$ $\implies 44 \times 5x^2 = 5500$ $\implies 220x^2 = 5500$
Step 3
Solve for $x$:
$x^2 = \dfrac{5500}{220} = 25$ $\implies x = \sqrt{25} = 5$.
Step 4
Find Radius $r$ and Height $h$:
Radius $r = 5x = 5 \times 5 = 25\text{ cm}$,   Height $h = 7x = 7 \times 5 = 35\text{ cm}$.
Answer: Radius of the cylinder $r = 25\text{ cm}$ and Height $h = 35\text{ cm}$.
3D Cylinder ($r:h = 5:7$, $\text{CSA} = 5500\text{ cm}^2$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 2 A solid iron cylinder has total surface area of $1848\text{ sq.cm}$. Its curved surface area is five – sixth of its total surface area. Find the radius and height of the iron cylinder.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 2 Solid Iron Cylinder
Must-Use Formula $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(h+r)$ $= 2\pi rh + 2\pi r^2$, $\text{CSA} = 2\pi rh$, $\text{TSA} - \text{CSA} = 2\pi r^2$
Step 1
Given Data: $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(h+r) = 1848\text{ cm}^2$.
$\text{CSA} = \dfrac{5}{6} \times \text{TSA}$ $= \dfrac{5}{6} \times 1848$ $= 5 \times 308 = 1540\text{ cm}^2$, so $2\pi rh = 1540$.
Step 2
Find Radius $r$ using $\text{TSA} - \text{CSA} = 2\pi r^2$:
$2\pi r^2 = 1848 - 1540 = 308$ $\implies 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times r^2 = 308$
Step 3
$r^2 = \dfrac{308 \times 7}{44} = 7 \times 7 = 49$ $\implies r = 7\text{ cm}$.
Step 4
Substitute $r = 7\text{ cm}$ in $2\pi rh = 1540$:
$2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times h = 1540$ $\implies 44h = 1540$ $\implies h = \dfrac{1540}{44} = 35\text{ cm}$.
Answer: Radius $r = 7\text{ cm}$ and Height $h = 35\text{ cm}$.
Solid Iron Cylinder ($r = 7\text{ cm}, h = 35\text{ cm}$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 3 The external radius and the length of a hollow wooden log are $16\text{ cm}$ and $13\text{ cm}$ respectively. If its thickness is $4\text{ cm}$ then find its T.S.A.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 3 Hollow Cylinder (Wooden Log)
Must-Use Formula TSA of Hollow Cylinder: $\text{TSA} = 2\pi(R + r)(R - r + h)\text{ sq. units}$, where $r = R - w$
Step 1
Given Dimensions: External radius $R = 16\text{ cm}$, length (height) $h = 13\text{ cm}$, thickness $w = 4\text{ cm}$.
Step 2
Find Internal Radius $r$:
$r = R - w = 16 - 4 = 12\text{ cm}$.
Step 3
Substitute into Hollow Cylinder TSA Formula:
$\text{TSA} = 2\pi(R + r)(R - r + h)$ $= 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times (16 + 12)(16 - 12 + 13)$
Step 4
Simplify & Multiply:
$\text{TSA} = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 28 \times 17$ $= 44 \times 4 \times 17 = 2992\text{ cm}^2$.
Answer: Total Surface Area (T.S.A.) of the hollow wooden log $= 2992\text{ sq.cm}$.
Hollow Wooden Log ($R=16, r=12, h=13\text{ cm}$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 4 A right angled triangle $PQR$ where $\angle Q = 90^\circ$ is rotated about $QR$ and $PQ$ as central axes. If $QR = 16\text{ cm}$ and $PR = 20\text{ cm}$, compare the curved surface areas of the right circular cones so formed by the triangle.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 4 Cone by Revolving Triangle
Must-Use Formula $PQ = \sqrt{PR^2 - QR^2}$ and CSA of Cone: $\text{CSA} = \pi rl\text{ sq. units}$
Step 1
Find side $PQ$ in right $\triangle PQR$:
Given $\angle Q = 90^\circ, QR = 16\text{ cm}$, hypotenuse $PR = l = 20\text{ cm}$.
$PQ = \sqrt{PR^2 - QR^2}$ $= \sqrt{20^2 - 16^2}$ $= \sqrt{400 - 256}$ $= \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ cm}$.
Step 2
Case (i) Rotated about $QR$ as axis:
Radius $r_1 = PQ = 12\text{ cm}$, height $h_1 = 16\text{ cm}$, slant height $l = 20\text{ cm}$.
$\text{CSA}_1 = \pi r_1 l$ $= \pi \times 12 \times 20$ $= 240\pi\text{ cm}^2$.
Step 3
Case (ii) Rotated about $PQ$ as axis:
Radius $r_2 = QR = 16\text{ cm}$, height $h_2 = 12\text{ cm}$, slant height $l = 20\text{ cm}$.
$\text{CSA}_2 = \pi r_2 l$ $= \pi \times 16 \times 20$ $= 320\pi\text{ cm}^2$.
Step 4
Comparison: Since $320\pi > 240\pi$, the CSA of the cone rotated about $PQ$ is larger than the CSA of the cone rotated about $QR$.
Answer: CSA of cone rotated about $PQ$ ($320\pi\text{ cm}^2$) is larger than CSA of cone rotated about $QR$ ($240\pi\text{ cm}^2$).
Right $\triangle PQR$ Revolved About $QR$ & $PQ$
Ex 7.1 - Q 5 $4$ persons live in a conical tent whose slant height is $19\text{ m}$. If each person require $22\text{ m}^2$ of the floor area, then find the height of the tent.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 5 Conical Tent
Must-Use Formula Base Area of Cone: $A = \pi r^2$ and Height: $h = \sqrt{l^2 - r^2}$
Step 1
Find Base Area of the Conical Tent:
Floor area for $4$ persons $= 4 \times 22 = 88\text{ m}^2$.
Step 2
Find $r^2$ from Base Area $\pi r^2 = 88$:
$\dfrac{22}{7} \times r^2 = 88$ $\implies r^2 = \dfrac{88 \times 7}{22}$ $= 4 \times 7 = 28$.
Step 3
Calculate Height $h$ using Slant Height $l = 19\text{ m}$:
$h = \sqrt{l^2 - r^2}$ $= \sqrt{19^2 - 28}$ $= \sqrt{361 - 28}$ $= \sqrt{333} \approx 18.25\text{ m}$.
Answer: Height of the conical tent $h = 18.25\text{ m}$.
Conical Tent ($l = 19\text{ m}$, Base $= 88\text{ m}^2$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 6 A girl wishes to prepare birthday caps in the form of right circular cones for her birthday party, using a sheet of paper whose area is $5720\text{ cm}^2$, how many caps can be made with radius $5\text{ cm}$ and height $12\text{ cm}$.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 6 Conical Birthday Caps
Must-Use Formula $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$, CSA of 1 Cap: $\pi rl$, No. of Caps: $\dfrac{\text{Total Area}}{\text{CSA of 1 Cap}}$
Step 1
Given Data: Radius $r = 5\text{ cm}$, height $h = 12\text{ cm}$, total sheet area $= 5720\text{ cm}^2$.
Step 2
Find Slant Height $l$:
$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$ $= \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2}$ $= \sqrt{25 + 144}$ $= \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ cm}$.
Step 3
Find CSA of $1$ Birthday Cap:
$\text{CSA} = \pi rl$ $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 5 \times 13$ $= \dfrac{1430}{7}\text{ cm}^2$.
Step 4
Calculate Number of Caps:
$\text{Number of caps}$ $= \dfrac{5720}{1430 / 7}$ $= \dfrac{5720 \times 7}{1430}$ $= 4 \times 7 = 28\text{ caps}$.
Answer: $28$ birthday caps can be made.
Conical Birthday Cap ($r=5, h=12, l=13\text{ cm}$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 7 The ratio of the radii of two right circular cones of same height is $1:3$. Find the ratio of their curved surface area when the height of each cone is $3$ times the radius of the smaller cone.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 7 Ratio of Two Cones
Must-Use Formula $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$ and $\dfrac{\text{CSA}_1}{\text{CSA}_2}$ $= \dfrac{\pi r_1 l_1}{\pi r_2 l_2}$ $= \dfrac{r_1 l_1}{r_2 l_2}$
Step 1
Set up Radii and Height:
Let $r_1 = x$ and $r_2 = 3x$. Given height $h = 3r_1 = 3x$ for both cones.
Step 2
Find Slant Heights $l_1$ and $l_2$:
$l_1 = \sqrt{r_1^2 + h^2}$ $= \sqrt{x^2 + (3x)^2}$ $= \sqrt{10x^2} = \sqrt{10}x$.
$l_2 = \sqrt{r_2^2 + h^2}$ $= \sqrt{(3x)^2 + (3x)^2}$ $= \sqrt{18x^2} = 3\sqrt{2}x$.
Step 3
Find Ratio of Curved Surface Areas:
$\dfrac{\text{CSA}_1}{\text{CSA}_2}$ $= \dfrac{\pi(x)(\sqrt{10}x)}{\pi(3x)(3\sqrt{2}x)}$ $= \dfrac{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{2}}{9\sqrt{2}}$ $= \dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{9}$.
Answer: Ratio of their curved surface areas $= \sqrt{5} : 9$.
Two Cones ($r_1:r_2 = 1:3$, Equal Height $h=3r_1$)
Ex 7.1 - Q 8 The radius of a sphere increases by $25\%$. Find the percentage increase in its surface area.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 8 Sphere Surface Area
Must-Use Formula Surface Area of Sphere: $S = 4\pi r^2$ and % Increase: $\dfrac{S_2 - S_1}{S_1} \times 100\%$
Step 1
Original Sphere: Let original radius be $r$.
Original surface area $S_1 = 4\pi r^2$.
Step 2
New Radius after $25\%$ Increase:
$r' = r + \dfrac{25}{100}r$ $= r + \dfrac{1}{4}r$ $= \dfrac{5}{4}r$.
Step 3
New Surface Area $S_2$:
$S_2 = 4\pi(r')^2$ $= 4\pi\left(\dfrac{5}{4}r\right)^2$ $= \dfrac{25}{16}(4\pi r^2)$ $= \dfrac{25}{16}S_1$.
Step 4
Percentage Increase in Surface Area:
$\%\text{ Increase}$ $= \dfrac{S_2 - S_1}{S_1} \times 100\%$ $= \left(\dfrac{25}{16} - 1\right) \times 100\%$ $= \dfrac{9}{16} \times 100\% = 56.25\%$.
Answer: Percentage increase in surface area $= 56.25\%$.
Sphere Radius $+25\% \implies$ Surface Area $+56.25\%$
Ex 7.1 - Q 9 The internal and external diameters of a hollow hemispherical vessel are $20\text{ cm}$ and $28\text{ cm}$ respectively. Find the cost to paint the vessel all over at ₹ $0.14\text{ per cm}^2$.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.1 Question 9 Hollow Hemispherical Vessel
Must-Use Formula TSA of Hollow Hemisphere: $\text{TSA} = \pi(3R^2 + r^2)\text{ sq. units}$
Step 1
Given Radii: Internal diameter $d = 20\text{ cm} \implies r = 10\text{ cm}$.
External diameter $D = 28\text{ cm} \implies R = 14\text{ cm}$.
Step 2
Total Surface Area to be Painted:
$\text{TSA} = \pi(3R^2 + r^2)$ $= \dfrac{22}{7}\left[3(14)^2 + 10^2\right]$
Step 3
$\text{TSA} = \dfrac{22}{7}(3 \times 196 + 100)$ $= \dfrac{22}{7}(588 + 100)$ $= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 688$ $= \dfrac{15136}{7}$ $\approx 2162.29\text{ cm}^2$.
Step 4
Calculate Total Painting Cost at ₹ $0.14\text{ per cm}^2$:
$\text{Cost} = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 688 \times 0.14$ $= 22 \times 688 \times 0.02 =$ ₹ $302.72$.
Answer: Total cost to paint the vessel $=$ ₹ $302.72$.
Hollow Hemispherical Vessel ($R=14\text{ cm}, r=10\text{ cm}$)

Ex 7.2 - Q 1 A $14\text{ m}$ deep well with inner diameter $10\text{ m}$ is dug and the earth taken out is evenly spread all around the well to form an embankment of width $5\text{ m}$. Find the height of the embankment.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.2 Question 1 Well & Embankment
Must-Use Formula Volume of Embankment: $\pi(R^2 - r^2)h_2$ $=$ Volume of Well: $\pi r^2 h_1$
Step 1
Given Dimensions: Well inner diameter $d = 10\text{ m} \implies r = 5\text{ m}$, depth $h_1 = 14\text{ m}$.
Embankment width $= 5\text{ m} \implies$ outer radius $R = 5 + 5 = 10\text{ m}$.
Step 2
Equate Volumes:
$\pi(R^2 - r^2)h_2 = \pi r^2 h_1$ $\implies (10^2 - 5^2)h_2 = 5^2 \times 14$
Step 3
Solve for Embankment Height $h_2$:
$(100 - 25)h_2 = 25 \times 14$ $\implies 75h_2 = 350$ $\implies h_2 = \dfrac{350}{75}$ $= \dfrac{14}{3} \approx 4.67\text{ m}$.
Answer: Height of the embankment $= 4.67\text{ m}$.
Well ($r=5, h_1=14\text{ m}$) & Embankment ($R=10\text{ m}$)

Ex 7.2 - Q 8 A solid sphere and a solid hemisphere have equal total surface area. Prove that the ratio of their volume is $3\sqrt{3} : 4$.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 7.2 Question 8 Sphere vs Hemisphere Proof
Must-Use Formula Sphere TSA: $4\pi r_1^2$, Hemisphere TSA: $3\pi r_2^2$, $\dfrac{V_1}{V_2}$ $= \dfrac{\frac{4}{3}\pi r_1^3}{\frac{2}{3}\pi r_2^3}$
Step 1
Equate Total Surface Areas: Let sphere radius be $r_1$ and hemisphere radius be $r_2$.
$4\pi r_1^2 = 3\pi r_2^2$ $\implies \dfrac{r_1^2}{r_2^2} = \dfrac{3}{4}$ $\implies \dfrac{r_1}{r_2} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
Step 2
Find Ratio of Volumes:
$\dfrac{V_1}{V_2}$ $= \dfrac{\frac{4}{3}\pi r_1^3}{\frac{2}{3}\pi r_2^3}$ $= 2\left(\dfrac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^3$
Step 3
Substitute $\dfrac{r_1}{r_2} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$:
$\dfrac{V_1}{V_2}$ $= 2 \times \left(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^3$ $= 2 \times \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{8}$ $= \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}$.
