Ex 8.1 - Q 1 Find the range and coefficient of range of the following data:

✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 8.1 Question 1 Range & Coeff. of Range
Must-Use Formula Range: $R = L - S$, Coefficient of Range: $\dfrac{L - S}{L + S}$
Part (i) Step 1
Given Data (i): $63, 89, 98, 125, 79, 108, 117, 68$.
Largest value $L = 125$,   Smallest value $S = 63$.
Part (i) Step 2
Range & Coefficient of Range (i):
$R = L - S = 125 - 63 = 62$.
$\text{Coefficient of Range} = \dfrac{L - S}{L + S} = \dfrac{125 - 63}{125 + 63} = \dfrac{62}{188} = \dfrac{31}{94} \approx 0.33$.
Part (ii) Step 3
Given Data (ii): $43.5, 13.6, 18.9, 38.4, 61.4, 29.8$.

Range & Coefficient of Range (ii):
$R = L - S = 61.4 - 13.6 = 47.8$.
$\text{Coefficient of Range} = \dfrac{61.4 - 13.6}{61.4 + 13.6} = \dfrac{47.8}{75} = \dfrac{478}{750} \approx 0.64$.
Answer: (i) $R = 62, \text{ Coeff. of Range} = 0.33$  |  (ii) $R = 47.8, \text{ Coeff. of Range} = 0.64$.

✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 8.1 Question 6 Wall Clock Strikes (24 Hours)
Must-Use Formula $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$, SD of First $n$ Even Numbers: $\sigma = 2\sqrt{\dfrac{n^2 - 1}{12}}$
Step 1

Number of strikes per hour number: $2, 4, 6, \dots, 24$ ($n = 12$ observations).

Find Standard Deviation of $2, 4, 6, \dots, 24$:

$\sigma = 2 \times \sqrt{\dfrac{n^2 - 1}{12}} = 2 \times \sqrt{\dfrac{12^2 - 1}{12}} = 2\sqrt{\dfrac{143}{12}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{143}{3}} = \sqrt{47.67} \approx 6.9$.
Answer: Total strikes in a day $= 156$ and Standard Deviation $\sigma \approx 6.9$.
