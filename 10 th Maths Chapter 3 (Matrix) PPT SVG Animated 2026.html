⭐ MUST-USE MATRICES FORMULA & PROPERTY CHEAT SHEET (CHAPTER 3 — PAGES 137 TO 154) Complete Matrix Reference
1. Order, Entries & Transpose (Ex 3.17) Order of $A$: $m \times n$ ($m$ rows, $n$ columns)  |  Total Entries: $mn$
Transpose $A^T$: Interchange rows & columns ($n \times m$), and $(A^T)^T = A$.
2. Special Types of Square Matrices ($m = n$) Diagonal: $a_{ij} = 0$ ($i \neq j$)  |  Scalar: $a_{ii} = k,\ a_{ij} = 0$ ($i \neq j$)
Identity ($I_n$): $a_{ii} = 1,\ a_{ij} = 0$ ($i \neq j$)  |  Triangular: Upper ($a_{ij}=0, i \gt j$), Lower ($a_{ij}=0, i \lt j$).
3. Matrix Addition & Scalar Multiplication (Ex 3.18) Commutative: $A + B = B + A$  |  Associative: $A + (B + C) = (A + B) + C$
Additive Identity: $A + O = A$  |  Additive Inverse: $A + (-A) = O$  |  Scalar: $kA = (ka_{ij})$.
4. Matrix Multiplication & Reversal Law (Ex 3.19) Order Rule: $A_{m \times n} \times B_{n \times p} = (AB)_{m \times p}$  |  In general: $AB \neq BA$
Associative: $(AB)C = A(BC)$  |  Distributive: $A(B+C) = AB + AC$  |  Transpose: $(AB)^T = B^T A^T$.

Ex 3.18 - Q 1 If $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 9 \\ 3 & 4 \\ 8 & -3 \end{pmatrix},\ B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 7 \\ 3 & 3 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ then verify that:
(i) $A + B = B + A$       (ii) $A + (-A) = (-A) + A = O$.
✍️ SOLUTION — Exercise 3.18 Question 1 (i) & (ii) Commutative & Additive Inverse Laws
Must-Use Properties (i) Commutative property of addition: $A + B = B + A$.  |  (ii) Additive inverse property: $A + (-A) = (-A) + A = O$.
Sub (i)
Verify $A + B = B + A$:
$$\text{LHS} = A + B = \begin{pmatrix} 1+5 & 9+7 \\ 3+3 & 4+3 \\ 8+1 & -3+0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 16 \\ 6 & 7 \\ 9 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$$ $$\text{RHS} = B + A = \begin{pmatrix} 5+1 & 7+9 \\ 3+3 & 3+4 \\ 1+8 & 0-3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 16 \\ 6 & 7 \\ 9 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$$
LHS $=$ RHS $\implies A + B = B + A$ verified.
Sub (ii)
Verify $A + (-A) = (-A) + A = O$:
Here $-A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -9 \\ -3 & -4 \\ -8 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.
$$A + (-A) = \begin{pmatrix} 1-1 & 9-9 \\ 3-3 & 4-4 \\ 8-8 & -3+3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = O$$ Similarly, $(-A) + A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = O$.
$A + (-A) = (-A) + A = O$ verified.
